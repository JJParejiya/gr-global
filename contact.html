Before you write

Questions we get asked most

If your question is not here, put it in the form above and we will answer it properly rather than with a brochure.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Within one business day for a standard air, ocean, road or rail consignment. Out-of-gauge or project cargo may take a little longer, because the route has to be surveyed before anyone can price it honestly.

What information do you need to quote?

The commodity, the gross weight and dimensions, the collection point and the destination. If you already know the incoterm and the sailing date you are aiming at, include those too and the quote comes back sharper.

Do you handle customs clearance as well as freight?

Yes. Classification, filing and duty assessment are handled at origin, and we brief the destination broker before the consignment lands so nothing sits waiting at the far end.

Is there a minimum shipment size?

No. Smaller orders are consolidated with other cargo so the unit cost stays sensible, rather than being turned away for being too small to be worth the paperwork.

Which countries do you ship to?

We regularly move freight to fifteen countries across Africa, Asia, the Americas and Europe. If your destination is not one we serve often, ask. We would rather tell you honestly than learn the route at your expense.

Can you store cargo before it sails?

Yes. Short and long-term storage is available between production and sailing, which avoids demurrage and stops you shipping a half-filled container just to meet a date.
