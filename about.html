Who we are

A freight desk in Bhuj, working to fifteen countries

GR Global was built on a simple idea. An exporter should be able to hand over a consignment and stop worrying about it. Everything we do follows from that.

Our story

Built around the consignment, not the paperwork

Setting up any export business is not an easy task. Between the vessel, the classification, the customs desk and the buyer waiting at the other end, most of the work happens in places the exporter never sees.

We started GR Global to take that entire middle section off our clients' desks. From our office on the Bhuj-Mirjapar Highway we book the space, prepare the documentation, clear the goods and follow the consignment until it is signed for.

Today we move air, ocean, road and rail freight for exporters working in agriculture, textiles, machinery, auto parts and eight other categories. Each one carries its own packing rules, its own classification and its own clearance requirements, and we hold that knowledge so our clients do not have to.

What has not changed is the way we work. One team, one point of contact, and a straight answer about where your cargo is.

How we work

Six commitments we hold to

Not a wall of promises. These are the things our clients actually hold us to, and the things we check ourselves against on every consignment.

  • Worldwide coverage

    Air, ocean, road and rail across Africa, Asia, the Americas and Europe, through a partner network we have used and trust.

  • Transparent tracking

    You are told where the consignment is and what happens next, without having to ask twice or chase a reference number.

  • One point of contact

    The same person handles your file from the quote to the delivery, so nothing is lost in a handover between departments.

  • Documentation done right

    Invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin and bills of lading prepared correctly the first time, because a rejected document is a delayed shipment.

  • Cargo treated as cargo

    Correct packing, correct classification and the right insurance cover, whether it is graded spices or a wheeled mining loader.

  • Round-the-clock support

    Vessels do not keep office hours and neither do problems. When something moves, you hear about it from us first.
