What we do

Four ways to move your cargo

Air, ocean, road and rail. Whichever route your consignment takes, the booking, the paperwork and the customs desk are handled by the same team.

Freight modes

Pick the route, we handle the rest

Fast transit times, smooth documentation and complete end-to-end support. Every consignment is managed with the same care, whichever way it travels.

Outstanding global coverage with expert air-freight knowledge

When the buyer is waiting and the margin sits in the calendar, cargo flies. Our worldwide air network provides rapid delivery at a cost that still makes sense, with smooth customs clearance and door-to-door transport at both ends.

Smooth sailing across seas from continent to continent

Water shipping is the core of import and export, and its advantage is reach. Printed materials, auto parts, electronic equipment, machinery and bulk agricultural goods all move by sea, and we book the space, seal the box and follow the vessel.

Reliable road movement from the factory gate to the port

Nothing reaches a port or an airport without a truck first. Our road network covers collection from the factory gate, movement between states and final distribution at destination, with the same visibility you get on the long-haul leg.

An extensive network of rails to reach any place in the world

For heavy loads over long distances, rail is the quiet workhorse. Secure rail transportation across Africa, Asia, the Americas and Europe, with our team monitoring the movement closely on routes that run for days rather than hours.

How it works

From enquiry to delivery in six steps

The same sequence runs on every consignment, whether it is one pallet of gift boxes or a wheeled loader on a flatbed.

  1. Enquiry and quote

    You tell us what is moving and where. We come back with a route, a transit time and a price, usually within one business day.

  2. Booking and space

    We reserve the space with the carrier, confirm the cut-off dates and lock in the rate before the market moves.

  3. Packing and collection

    Cargo is packed to the standard the route requires, then collected from your gate and delivered to the port, airport or terminal.

  4. Documentation

    Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and transport document prepared and checked, because a rejected paper is a delayed shipment.

  5. Customs clearance

    Classification, filing and duty handled at origin, with the destination broker briefed before the consignment lands.

  6. Transit and delivery

    You get position updates while it moves, and confirmation with proof of delivery once the buyer has signed for it.

Everything else

The work around the freight

Moving the box is the visible part. These are the services that decide whether it arrives on time and at the price you were quoted.

  • Customs clearance

    Correct classification, filing and duty assessment at origin and destination, handled by people who deal with the desk every day.

  • Export documentation

    Invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, inspection certificates and transport documents prepared and checked before submission.

  • Warehousing and storage

    Short and long-term storage between production and sailing, so you are not paying demurrage or rushing a half-filled container.

  • Cargo insurance

    Cover arranged against the real risks of the route, with a clear explanation of what is and is not included before you commit.

  • Tracking and reporting

    Position updates at every milestone, plus consolidated reporting if you are running more than a handful of shipments a month.

  • Packing and consolidation

    Export-grade packing, palletising and crating, with consolidation of smaller orders into one box to bring the unit cost down.
